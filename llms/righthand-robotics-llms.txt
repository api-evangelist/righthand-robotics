# RightHand Robotics

> RightHand Robotics builds autonomous piece-picking robotic systems (the RightPick platform) that use computer vision, machine learning, and adaptive grasping to pick and place millions of SKUs for order fulfillment across e-commerce, pharmaceutical, apparel, health & beauty, and grocery operations. There is no public web API; integration and deployment documentation is provided to authorized customers and partners through a login-gated partner portal.

## Company
- [Website](https://righthandrobotics.com): Company site, products, and applications
- [Partner Documentation Portal](https://www.rhrdocs.com): Login-gated product, deployment, integration, and operator documentation for authorized customers and partners
- [News & Blog](https://righthandrobotics.com/the-latest): Dated company news, blog, and press posts
- [Resources](https://righthandrobotics.com/resources): Case studies and white papers
- [Contact / Request a Demo](https://righthandrobotics.com/contact-us): Sales and support contact
- [Careers](https://righthandrobotics.com/careers): Open positions
- [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/RightHandRobotics): Open-source ROS packages and C control libraries for the ReFlex robotic hand hardware

## Security
- [security.txt](https://righthandrobotics.com/.well-known/security.txt): RFC 9116 security contact and disclosure policy

## Notes
- No public REST/GraphQL/AsyncAPI, OpenAPI specification, SDK, or MCP server is published.
- The GitHub organization hosts hardware/robotics libraries (ROS packages, ReFlex Hand C API) rather than web-API client SDKs.
